# Grand Arena Championship (GAC) — Complete Mechanics

*Compiled July 2026. Banner values reflect the post-2021 scoring system verified
against a Capital Games (EA_HighCommand) dev post. Squad counts and skill-rating
point values are league/division-dependent and not fully published — confirm on
your live GAC map.*

---

## 1. What GAC Is

Scheduled **1-vs-1 PvP**. Requires **Player Level 85**. You set defensive teams,
then attack the opponent's.

**Structure:** Season = four 1-week **events**; each event = **3 rounds** vs a
different opponent; each round = **24h Defense Phase** + **24h Attack Phase**. A
season = 12 head-to-head matches.

**vs Territory Wars:** GAC is 1v1 (TW is guild-vs-guild). **In GAC you earn
banners on offense only** — defense does not generate banners, it only *denies*
them. GAC has a persistent ladder (Leagues/Divisions/Skill Rating); TW doesn't.

---

## 2. Formats: 5v5 vs 3v3 (and Fleets)

Each player controls **4 zones/territories**. Zones run as **5v5** (teams up to 5)
or **3v3** (teams up to 3); the format is announced per season. An event may or may
not include a **fleet zone** (Kyber league historically gets an extra defensive
fleet).

- **"Up to" matters** — you may field *fewer* units than the cap (an "undersized"
  squad); this is a core scoring lever (§4).
- The **number of defensive squads/fleets scales with your League** and is shown in
  the corner of each territory on the live map. Read it off your board.

---

## 3. Board & Deployment Flow

1. **Defense Phase:** fill required defensive slots; deployed units are **locked**
   for the round (can't attack with them).
2. **Attack Phase:** attack the opponent's defense to conquer territories; they do
   the same to you. Front zones open first; back zones unlock as fronts clear.
3. **Result:** most total **banners** at round end wins the round.

Because banners come only from offense, strong defense wins by making the opponent
**spend more units / lose more banners** than you do.

---

## 4. Scoring: Banners in Detail ⭐

Banners are earned **per attack battle, on offense only**. Current formula (dev
EA_HighCommand): ([dev tracker](https://devtrackers.gg/swgoh/p/1f896cbc-new-gac-attack-points-scoring-incorrect-in-game))

| Component | Banners |
|---|---|
| **Win** the battle | **+15** |
| **First-try bonus** (clear on first attempt) | **+30** |
| Per **surviving** unit | +1 each |
| Per survivor at **full health** | +1 each |
| Per survivor at **full protection** | +1 each |
| Per **unused squad slot** (undersizing) | **+4** each |
| Per **enemy defeated** | +1 each |

**Worked (5v5):** full team, all survive full HP/prot, first try, 5 kills =
15+30+5+5+5+0+5 = **65**. Solo clear of a 5-enemy team, pristine, first try =
15+30+1+1+1+(4×4)+5 = **69** — the **character-battle max**.

### The key levers
- **Undersizing (+4/empty slot):** a solo/duo clear earns *more* than a full-team
  clear of the same enemy — the basis of "banner efficiency." Deep relic rosters
  undersize to squeeze extra banners.
- **Defense earns you ZERO banners.** Its only value is **denying** the opponent
  (a hold = they score 0 there) or **banner-stripping** (killing some attackers to
  shrink their survivor/HP/protection bonuses). **There is NO "successful-defend"
  bonus** — holding doesn't add to *your* score, it subtracts from *theirs*.

### Why two full clears differ by ~1 point
Both near the cap → decided by efficiency: a **unit that died** (−up to 3), a
survivor **not at full health/protection** (−1 each), **fewer undersized slots**
(missed +4s), or needing a **second attempt** (loses the **+30 first-try** — the
biggest swing). It's efficiency, not luck.

### Exact-tie tiebreaker
Equal banners → higher **locked Galactic Power** wins. Equal banners *and* GP →
**random**. ([EA Forums](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/swgoh-general-discussion-en/gac-tiebreaker/3931542))

## 5. First-Mover Myth — Debunked

**No mechanical first-move bonus.** Attacking first awards no extra banners. The
only difference is **information** (attack second → you see what you need) vs
**pressure** (attack first → set the target). Strategic trade-off, not a scoring
rule. ([EA Forums](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/swgoh-general-discussion-en/gac-attacking-first/4480661))

## 6. Matchmaking, Leagues, Divisions & Skill Rating

- **Matchmaking:** Round 1 by **GP**; Rounds 2–3 by **win/loss record** that week.
- **Leagues (low→high):** Carbonite → Bronzium → Chromium → Aurodium → **Kyber**.
  5 leagues × **5 divisions** each.
- **Skill Rating** (hidden) sets your Division. Initialized from your **GP at Level
  85**. **Winning rounds raises it; losing lowers it.** Skipping an event costs
  Skill Rating **as if you lost two matches**. Since Nov 2021, **Feats no longer
  affect promotion** — ladder movement is driven by **win/loss**. Exact point
  values are unpublished/variable.
- **Season reward eligibility:** earn **≥20 banners** across the season.

---

## 7. Rewards & Why GAC Matters

Pays **daily** (by League/Division), **per-round** (winner & loser both get
prizes incl. **Crystals**), and **per-season**. Bulk payout is **Championship
Tokens** → **Championship Store** (a top source of **gear** and **mod-slicing
materials**). Also grants Mod Salvage, Ability Mats, Credits, Crystals.

> **Naming:** the GAC currency is officially **Championship Tokens**. "Aurodium"
> and "Kyber" are **League names**, not currencies; "Get 1/Get 2" is community
> slang. The "Kyber Lightspeed Token" is a separate promo item.

**Why it's the hub:** steady Crystal income (funds refreshes/shards), key mod-
slicing + gear source, and it rewards **roster breadth/depth** (3v3 + undersizing)
— which compounds across the whole account.
