# Fleets / Ships — Mechanics + Capital Ship Meta

*Compiled July 2026. The tier list and top-fleet sections are time-sensitive
(2025–2026 meta) — verify live win rates on swgoh.gg. Several primary sources were
read via search summaries; single-source claims flagged **[unverified]**.*

---

## 1. Fleet Composition

| Element | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Capital Ship | 1 | Always on the field; anchors the fleet, provides fleet-wide abilities + an ultimate |
| Starting ships | **2** | Deploy alongside the capital ship at start |
| Reinforcements | **3** | Held in reserve, called in one at a time |
| **Total active** | **6** (1 capital + 5 standard) | |

> ⚠️ **Correction to a common mistake:** a fleet is **1 capital + 2 starters + 3
> reinforcements**, *not* "3 starters + 4 reinforcements." Reserve ships aren't on
> the board; the enemy only has to destroy deployed ships.

**Fleet Arena vs GAC:** Fleet Arena uses this composition (post–"Ships 2.0", it
plays as a fast 3-ships-per-side dogfight). GAC's **5v5/3v3 label applies to
character squads, not fleets** [unverified against a single page] — the GAC fleet
zone (when present) uses the same standard fleet format, and an event may run with
or without a ship territory.

## 2. Reinforcements & Battle Flow

- Opens with capital + 2 starters; field holds up to **3 standard ships/side**.
- **Reinforcements** are called in one at a time to fill open slots; each has a
  **Reinforcement (RI) on-enter ability** (e.g., Kylo's Command Shuttle strips 10%
  enemy TM at RI1 / 20% at RI2; others grant ally TM). Choosing *which* ship enters
  *when* is the core of fleet strategy.
- **Timing:** the **3rd reinforcement and the capital ultimate arrive at ~the same
  point**; most fights end before then, so **don't put your strongest ship in the
  3rd (last) reinforcement slot**.
- Turn order is **speed/turn-meter driven**, which is why TM-gain and TM-removal
  reinforcements are so valuable.

## 3. Ship Stats Come From the CREW (pilots) ⭐

The single most important ship investment concept:

- **A crewed ship's stats come almost entirely from its crew.** You can't upgrade a
  ship's stats directly — you raise each **pilot's** gear/**relic**/star/level/
  ability, and the ship converts that (via hidden crew-contribution multipliers)
  into ship stats.
- **Relics matter a lot:** R1–3 modest, R4–6 significant, **R7+ the biggest
  boosts**. Endgame fleets want key pilots at high relic. *(This is why our
  player's IG-88 at R2 drags IG-2000 down.)*
- **Mods work differently for ships:** only the mod's **tier and level** count —
  primary/secondary/set bonuses are **ignored** for ship stats. So pilots need
  high-tier, high-level mods, not well-rolled ones.

**Bottom line:** pilot investment (especially relics/gear) is the primary lever on
crewed-ship power. Same ship, G13/R7 crew ≫ G8 crew.

---

## 4. Crewed vs Crewless

- **Crewed** — stats scale off crew (most standard ships).
- **Crewless** — no pilots; stats fixed by the ship's own star/level/abilities
  (many droid ships; some newer journey ships). Some kits key off the crewless tag
  (e.g., Profundity gives "crewless Rebel tanks" crit immunity).
- **Capital ships** — no crew; power from **star level + ability levels**.

---

## 5. Capital Ship Abilities & Leveling

- Capital/ship abilities upgrade with **ship ability materials** (Omega to lvl 7→8;
  a full **ship Zeta = 20 Zeta mats**), from the **Ship Ability Materials
  Challenge** (Fleet challenge). Reinforcement (RI) mats upgrade on-enter abilities
  (most want **RI level 3**).
- **Why maxing matters:** a capital ship's defining mechanics (fleet buffs, taunts,
  damage immunity, its ultimate, RI effects) only hit full strength at max level. An
  under-leveled capital ship is often the difference between a hold and a loss.
  *(This is exactly our player's Profundity issue — Holdo Maneuver at 2/8.)*

---

## 6. Rewards & Crystal Income

- **Fleet Arena:** daily crystal payout by **rank at your payout time** (roughly
  ~500 at rank 1 sliding to a low-rank floor — check the in-game **Prizes tab** for
  exact current numbers [unverified table]). Also grants Fleet Arena currency.
  A high fleet rank is one of the steadiest F2P crystal sources.
- **GAC fleet zone:** no separate fleet reward track; the fleet contributes banners
  toward overall GAC scoring → League/Division/round/season rewards.

## 7. Capital Ship Tier List — 2025–2026 (time-sensitive)

The fleet meta is heavily **rock-paper-scissors**; the top journey ships (Leviathan,
Executor, Profundity) must be unlocked via the Journey Guide. **Cross-check live at
[swgoh.gg ship counters](https://swgoh.gg/gac/ship-counters/).**

| Tier | Capital (fleet) | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **S+** | **Leviathan** (Sith) | The dominant defensive anchor — beats most fleets outright. The bar to beat. |
| **S** | **Executor** (Empire) | Top offense + premier Leviathan counter; a rework pushed it past Profundity in mirrors [rework unverified]. |
| **S** | **Profundity** (Rebel/Raddus + Bounty Hunters) | Reliable, flexible counter and defense; kept competitive by a 2025 buff. |
| **A** | **Endurance** (Rebel) | Old basic ship that is the **highest-banner Leviathan counter** when piloted right — high skill ceiling, low avg win rate (~34%). |
| **A** | **Malevolence** (Separatist) | One of the best pure attackers; slow shard farm. |
| **A** | **Negotiator** (Galactic Republic) | Extremely balanced/forgiving; strong counter and ladder-climber. |
| **B** | **Executrix** (Empire) | F2P offense; still counter tech (Endurance+Executrix+Profundity clears all 3 journey ships ~76 banners). |
| **B** | **Chimaera** (Empire/Thrawn) | Solid, easy to obtain; falls off at the top. |
| **B** | **Home One** (Rebel/Ackbar) | Good early/mid, easy build; niche counter. |
| **C** | **Finalizer** (First Order) | Anchors FO fleets; aging/niche. **(But note: it's SLKR's required capital ship.)** |

**Read:** Leviathan is apex on defense; **Executor, Profundity, Endurance** are the
primary answers — those four dominate high-level fleet play.

## 8. Top Meta Fleets (key ships)

- **Leviathan** (Sith) — current top defense, punishing on auto.
- **Executor** (Empire) — top offense + Leviathan counter (a known line runs MK-6
  as first reinforcement for ~83% vs Leviathan).
- **Profundity + Bounty Hunters** (Raddus) — crewless Rebel tanks (crit immunity) +
  BH reinforcements; reliable both ways.
- **Endurance** (Rebel) — the skill-intensive anti-Leviathan fleet.
- **Negotiator + Galactic Republic** — balanced, forgiving ladder-climber.
- **Malevolence + Separatist droids** — aggressive burst.

A representative high-end **defensive set**: **Leviathan + Executor + Profundity.**
