## 1. What Galactic Legends (GLs) Are

The **most powerful character class** — a tier above Legendary/Journey units,
unlocked only via **Galactic Ascension** events, designed as win-conditions in
**GAC** and **Territory War**.

Why they dominate:
- Kits scale off huge stat pools and unique mechanics (Ultimate charge, Siphon/
  health-steal, Isolate, Protection Up) normal units lack.
- **They win outnumbered** — a built GL can beat full 5-unit squads → elite on
  **offense** (clearing for banners) *and* **defense** (forces the opponent to
  spend their own GL or an exact counter). Since clears/holds are the currency of
  GAC/TW, more (and the *right*) GLs is the single biggest lever on placement.

**[CHECK IN-GAME — meta]** By mid-2026 the GL roster is large. Rey and SLKR (the
first two, 2020) are now widely treated as **entry-level/budget GLs** —
power-crept by later releases (Jabba, Lord Vader, JML, Sith Eternal Emperor, Leia,
etc.). Verify current rankings on [swgoh.gg GAC](https://swgoh.gg/gac/) or a
current tier list.

## 2. Galactic Ascension — How Unlocking Works

([swgoh.wiki: Galactic Ascension](https://swgoh.wiki/wiki/Galactic_Ascension))

- GLs release in **Light Side / Dark Side pairs**.
- Events live in the **Journey Guide**, enterable any time **after prerequisites
  are met**.
- **Prerequisites are a roster gate:** specific characters (+ often a ship) at
  required **Relic/Star** levels. Requirement lists run **13+ units** for newer
  GLs. Originals (Rey/SLKR): **Lvl 85, 12 chars @ Relic 3+, 1 capital ship**.
  Newer GLs demand more (e.g. 14 @ R5+, some R7).

**Tier structure:** Tiers 1–3 give **character shards** (repeatable); Tier 4
Title; Tier 5 Portrait; Tier 6 the **Ultimate Ability** materials (repeatable).

**Getting to 330 shards** (unlock) — repeat Tiers 1–3, paying alignment currency:

| Tier | Attempts × cost | Currency | Shards |
|------|-----------------|----------|--------|
| 1 | 8 × 15 | 120 | ~80 **[UNVERIFIED split]** |
| 2 | 4 × 30 | 120 | ~100 **[UNVERIFIED split]** |
| 3 | 3 × 60 | 180 | ~150 **[UNVERIFIED split]** |
| **Total** | | **~420** | **330** |

The **8×15 / 4×30 / 3×60** schedule is well-corroborated; the shard split is
inferred — confirm live. **Failing a battle still consumes tickets.** Battle
faction lock: **Rey → Resistance only**, **SLKR → First Order only**.

## 3. THE TICKET / ENERGY ECONOMY (the misunderstood part)

The real "cost" of a GL is an **energy-based drop system**, not a shop purchase.

**How you earn currency:**
- **Light Side Currency drops from Light Side Battles; Dark Side Currency from
  Dark Side Battles** — you earn it by **spending regular energy** on the matching
  alignment's campaign nodes. ([LS Currency](https://swgoh.wiki/wiki/Light_Side_Currency) / [DS Currency](https://swgoh.wiki/wiki/Dark_Side_Currency))
- **Drops only start once you meet the GL's prerequisites.** Before that you earn
  **zero**, even while spending energy there.
- Small amounts also come from Shard Shop / Guild Store, but node farming is the
  main faucet.

**The 144 cap + two SEPARATE pools:**
- Each currency **caps at 144**; drops won't push you over — you must spend to keep
  earning.
- **Light and Dark are completely separate pools** — grinding one does nothing for
  the other.

**The catch — both pools drain the SAME regular energy:**
- Light and Dark **Battles both run on your single shared regular energy** bar
  (144 cap, ~14h24m refill; crystal-refreshable up to 2000/day). ([Energy](https://swgoh.wiki/wiki/Energy))
- → The two ticket pools **compete with each other** for one finite energy budget,
  **and** compete with **gear farming** (nodes also drop gear/salvage). Chasing a
  GL means diverting most of your daily energy to one alignment for weeks, at the
  cost of the other alignment's tickets and of gear progress.

**Farm smart:**
- **Double-dip nodes** — where tickets come with gear you need. Commonly cited:
  **DS node 8A** (and **9C**) for Kyrotech gear + Dark tickets.
- **Hammer double-drop events** (2× rewards/energy); bank crystal refreshes for
  those windows.
- **Spend down toward the cap before refreshing** so no drops are wasted.

## 4. How the Economy Shapes Which GL to Chase

- **Same-side GLs compete for the same ticket pool** (e.g., SLKR and Lord Vader
  both need Dark currency) — finish one, spend down, then start the next.
- **Opposite-side GLs don't share a ticket pool** (Rey LS vs SLKR DS accumulate
  independently)…
- **…but every GL still draws from the same regular energy**, so even
  opposite-side GLs indirectly compete (and with gear).
- **Practical:** commit to **one alignment at a time** for ticket farming, sequence
  GLs deliberately, exploit double-drops, don't start a second same-side grind
  until the first is unlocked and spent down.

## 6. Cheat Sheet
- **GL = strongest class**, wins outnumbered → elite offense *and* defense.
- **Unlock = Galactic Ascension:** meet a 13+ unit relic/star gate, repeat Tiers
  1–3 for **330 shards**; Tier 6 = Ultimate.
- **Ticket cost:** ~420 alignment currency (8×15 + 4×30 + 3×60).
- **Currency = energy-based drops** on LS/DS nodes, only **after prerequisites**.
- **Cap 144/side; LS & DS separate;** both drain your **shared regular energy** and
  compete with **gear farming**.
- **Farm:** double-dip nodes (8A/9C), double-drop events, spend down before
  capping. Same-side GLs share a pool → one at a time.
