# Currencies, Stores & the Resource Economy

*Source-verified, current as of July 2026. The economy changed a lot with the
**10-Year Anniversary "Era" overhaul (Nov 2025)** and later patches; exact numbers
shift — verified figures cited inline, unconfirmed ones flagged **⚠️**.*

---

## 1. Crystals — the Premium Currency

Crystals gate the fastest progression (energy refreshes, packs, unlocks), so
**crystal income is the most important long-term economic lever.**

**Main sources:** GAC daily reward (biggest rank-based stream since Nov 2021; min
55/day, scales by League/Division) · **Fleet Arena daily payout** (~500 at rank 1
sliding down) · daily activities (~65) · Galactic War (~25) · login (~500/mo) ·
Territory Battles · achievements/quests (big one-time dumps) · events.

> ⚠️ **Correction:** **Squad Arena no longer pays crystals** — removed in the Nov
> 2021 revamp and moved to GAC. The modern rule is *"hold high **Fleet Arena** and
> **GAC** rank for daily crystals."* Squad Arena rank now pays **Squad Arena
> tokens**, not crystals.

**Why rank compounds:** higher Fleet/GAC rank = hundreds more crystals/day = more
energy refreshes = faster farms = higher rank. Defend rank aggressively.

**Smart spending:** cheap **energy refreshes** are the best routine buy (§2). Avoid
random gear/shard packs. Save bursts for **event enablers**.

## 2. Energy & Refreshes

| Energy | Feeds |
|---|---|
| **Regular/Normal** | Light & Dark side battles — main gear/shard + **GL ticket** farms |
| **Cantina** | Cantina nodes (shards, ability mats, Signal Data) |
| **Ship/Fleet** | Ship battles/shards |
| **Mod** | Mod battles |
| **Conquest** | Conquest (event-scoped) |

- **Free daily energy:** multiple scheduled drops + daily reset (collect within the
  window). ⚠️ Exact amounts vary by version.
- **Paid refreshes (crystals):** Regular Energy escalates **50 / 50 / 100 / 100 /
  200 / 200**, daily limit, resets at daily reset. **The two 50-crystal refreshes
  are among the best buys in the game** — they ~double daily farming cheaply.
- **Never let energy overflow** — spend down before the free drops.

## 3. Stores, Currencies & Best Buys

| Store | Currency | Best buys / priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Squad Arena** | Squad Arena tokens | Character shards → then keep buying shards (→ Shard Shop currency) |
| **Fleet Arena** | Fleet Arena tokens | Ships/pilots, then **Zeta mats forever** (a primary F2P Zeta source) |
| **Cantina** | Cantina tokens | Unowned character shards; then dump on Cassian's U-Wing (best Shard-Shop conversion) |
| **Guild Store** | Guild + Raid (Mk I/II/III) tokens | **Character shards (Mk III raid tokens)**; gear salvage |
| **Guild Events (GET1/2/3)** | Guild Event Tokens | **GET3 → Kyrotech** (biggest modern gear bottleneck); GET2/3 → Dark Side Kyro |
| **Galactic War** | GW tokens | Characters first, then ships forever (Shard-Shop conversion) |
| **Shard Shop** | Shard currency | **G12 purple/yellow gear** (4 for 360); stun guns/cuffs/carbantis/holo projectors |
| **GAC / Championship** | Championship Tokens (league-tier) | **Omicron (Mk3), Omega (Mk1), Dark Side Kyro, mod-slicing mats** — a rare F2P omicron source |
| **Conquest** | Conquest Credits | Conquest-exclusive Tech/consumables, gear, Signal Data, relic mats, exclusive units |
| **Era Shipment** | **Era Currency** | **Lightspeed Tokens** + **Relic-tier gear mats** (§5) |

---

## 4. Conquest (Overview)

Recurring solo event; a top source of **exclusive characters, gear, and currency**.
Spend **Conquest Energy** through Sectors, picking **Data Disks** (buffs) per run.
Battles + **Feats** award **Keycards** → end-of-event **Reward Crate** tier. Also
earns **Conquest Credits** for the store. Often the **gate to meta / GL-enabler
characters** — high crate tiers are a priority.

---

## 5. Era Currency & Lightspeed Tokens

The **Era overhaul (Nov 2025)** restructured progression around **Eras** (Episodes,
Marquees, Era Journey Guide, Era Challenges) + the **Coliseum** leaderboard.

**Era Currency (EC)** is earned from **Era-exclusive modes**: Coliseum, Episode
Track milestones, Era Challenges, Era Marquees, Era Journey Guide. Spent in the
**Era Shipment** store on:

- **Lightspeed Tokens (LSTs)** — instantly upgrade an *owned, eligible* unit to a
  set baseline (five tiers: **Carbonite → Bronzium → Chromium → Aurodium → Kyber**;
  see `04-progression.md` for exact baselines). ⚠️ tier stats were rebalanced at the
  anniversary — verify in-game.
- **Relic-tier gear materials** (a key end-game relic sink).

**Confirmed (Community Update 4-27-2026):** Aurodium LST **reduced 5,000 → 3,500
EC**; devs targeted **≥200% more EC inflow**.

**Cap:** the in-game **Era Currency screen shows a 150,000 cap** (confirmed from a
player screenshot: "34,704 / 150,000"). The predecessor Episode Currency capped at
100,000.

**Spending guidance:** EC is Era-scoped and converts *unfavorably* at Era's end —
**don't hoard toward the cap.** Spend on LSTs (jump a meta squad to relevance) or
Relic mats. For LSTs, always check the token's **eligible-unit list** first (fixed
at release, faction/era-restricted, never expanded).

## 6. Resource Priority — Mid-Game Playbook

1. **Cheap daily energy refreshes** (50-crystal Regular tiers + daily Cantina/Fleet)
   — highest-ROI routine spend; multiplies every farm.
2. **Defend Fleet Arena rank + climb GAC** → more daily crystals (compounds #1).
3. **Gear bottlenecks over shards** — Kyrotech (GET3), stun guns/cuffs/carbantis
   (Shard Shop), Signal Data & relic mats are the real chokepoints.
4. **Zetas (Fleet store) + Omicrons (GAC/Conquest/Era)** — the PvP-swinging upgrades.
5. **Conquest to the highest crate** for exclusive units/gear.
6. **Store shard-buy → Shard Shop conversion loop** once a store's characters are done.
7. **Era Currency → LSTs / Relic mats**, spent (not hoarded) before Era end.
8. **Save crystal bursts for event enablers**, not impulse packs.

**One-liner:** *Turn crystals into cheap energy refreshes, energy into gear/shard
progress, rank into more crystals — spend store tokens on the bottleneck (gear,
kyro, zetas, omicrons), not the easy stuff.*
