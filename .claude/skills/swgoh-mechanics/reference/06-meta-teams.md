# GAC Meta — Defense & Offense Teams (2025–2026)

*Last researched July 19, 2026. **DATE-SENSITIVE:** the GAC meta shifts every
marquee/GL release and balance pass. Any team/ranking here can be stale within 1–2
months — re-verify on [swgoh.gg/gac](https://swgoh.gg/gac/) before spending
resources. Some figures came from search summaries; treat exact win/hold rates as
approximate.*

---

## 1. What Makes a Team "Hold" on Defense

Defensive teams are run by the **AI**, so a good wall is one the AI pilots
competently *and* that punishes or outlasts the attacker:

- **Revive/summon stall** — repeatedly bringing units back / spawning bodies drags
  the fight to the timer (Nightsisters: Zombie/Daka revives, Talzin spirits).
- **Sustain & damage-gating** — kits that make a unit un-killable until a condition
  is met force an exact kill order; miss it and you lose (General Skywalker/501st,
  Bo-Katan Mand'alor, Jabba).
- **Counters / out-of-turn punishment** — counterattacks, assists, TM gain on hit
  (Inquisitors, Imperial Troopers under Iden, Sith).
- **Crowd control / TM denial** — stuns, ability block, buff immunity, TM removal.
- **AI-friendliness** — a kit that needs precise manual play makes a poor wall; the
  best defenses do the right thing on auto.

**Why GLs are the best walls:** highest stats, deep innate protection, and factions
built around them — they force the attacker to bring a *specific* GL counter and
pilot it right, costing banners and roster depth.

## 2. Best Non-GL Defensive Teams

| Team (Leader) | Roster | Why it holds / current strength |
|---|---|---|
| **Great Mothers Nightsisters** ⭐ | Great Mothers (L), Merrin, Mother Talzin, Old Daka, Nightsister Zombie | **Top-tier non-GL wall.** Revive/summon stall + automatic bonus turns (Fate Weaving); NS get +30% Def/Health, +30 Speed. Best 5 varies (Asajj, Morgan Elsbeth, Night/Death Trooper also seen in GM cores). Most counters are GLs. |
| **General Skywalker / 501st** | GAS (L) + Rex, Fives, Echo, ARC | Damage-gating "mini raid boss" — GAS un-damageable while 501st live; strict kill order. Frustrating wall if attacker doesn't know it. **Needs GAS.** |
| **Bo-Katan (Mand'alor) Mandalorians** | BK Mand'alor (L), The Mandalorian, Paz Vizsla, BK Kryze, IG-12 & Grogu | Stacks huge Defense per debuff / per living Mando ally; heals on first ally death. Solid, flexible banner-bleed wall. **Needs BK Mand'alor.** |
| **Galactic Republic — Padmé** | Padmé (L), JKA, General Kenobi, Ahsoka (+ C-3PO / Commander Ahsoka) | Protection-up loops, buff immunity, sustain — hard for damage-reliant teams to break. Durable, long-standing wall. |
| **Sith Empire — Malgus** | Darth Malgus (L), Darth Revan, Bastila (Fallen), Malak, Darth Talon | Sith get big Mastery/Speed/Crit-Avoid/DR in GAC; forces strong counters. Stable banner-bleed wall. |
| **Inquisitors** | Third Sister/Grand Inquisitor (L) + Inquisitors | Out-of-turn punishment, CC. Solid **mid-tier** wall — top teams beat it often enough; treat as "bleed banners," not a hard lock. |
| **Clones — Rex lead (no GAS)** | Rex (L), Fives, Echo, + sustain | Sustain/heal loops; a cheaper clone wall. |
| **Imperial Troopers (Iden)** | Iden (L), Veers, Range/Shore/Death Trooper | Out-of-turn offense engine — best as **aggro/offense**; a mid wall on defense. |
| ~~**Bounty Hunters (Bossk)**~~ | Bossk (L) + BH | ⚠️ **Weak on DEFENSE** — Bossk needs taunt up immediately and gives no opening speed, so he gets CC'd and bursted. **Use BH on OFFENSE instead.** |

**Mid-game read:** **Great Mothers Nightsisters is the premier *achievable* non-GL
wall**, then GAS/501st (if you have GAS) and Malgus Sith / Bo-Katan Mandalorians as
banner-bleed walls. Jabba and GL Leia are elite but are GLs.

> **Correction vs earlier advice:** Bounty Hunters were previously suggested here as
> a top defensive wall — community data says the opposite (weak on defense, strong
> on offense). Padmé, Malgus, Bo-Katan, and Great Mothers are the better *walls*.

## 3. Best GAC Offense / Counters (Mid-Game)

You win on **offense** by matching the right counter to each defensive team, not by
fielding one super-squad.

- **Use live counter tools** — [swgoh.gg/gac/counters](https://swgoh.gg/gac/counters/)
  and [swgoh-cantina counters](https://swgoh-cantina.com/en/gac-counters) — filter
  to your roster; counters change weekly.
- **Great Mothers counters** (high win rates cited): The Stranger (~99%), Third
  Sister squads (~97%), Leia teams (~95%), Jedi GLs (JML/JKR/GAS).
- **GAS/501st:** kill order **Fives → Rex → Echo → any → GS** (never a random clone
  first — triggers Fives' sacrifice).
- **Strong aggro/offense squads:** Imperial Troopers (Iden), Bounty Hunters
  (Bossk/Fennec counter-comps), **JMK** (Jedi Master Kenobi) cores.
- **Fleet:** **Profundity** counters both Executor variants.

## 4. Galactic Legend Priority (Mid-Game, 2026)

Highest-impact single investment for GAC. Rough current standing — **verify before
gearing:**

| GL | Standing | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **SLKR** | **Top / best overall** | Reportedly still the best GL; beats other "ultimate" GLs 1v1, huge offense flexibility. |
| **GL Rey** | **Top offense** | The premier *Legend killer* / offensive pick. |
| **Jabba the Hutt** | **Top-tier, all modes** | Excels PvE + PvP; anchors the best BH/Hutt defensive team. Great value. |
| **GL Leia** | **Strong, rising** | Rebel lead "amazing on both offense and defense." |
| **JML** | **Very strong, versatile** | Leads Jedi *and* Light Side; near-universal utility. |
| **SEE (Sith Eternal Emperor)** | **Down but usable** | Former premier wall; struggles vs newer teams. Lower priority. |
| **GL Ahsoka** | **Weak-ish / divisive** | Loses to many GAC teams, poor on defense. Lower priority; rough launch. |

**Guidance:** favor a GL strong on **offense across many matchups** and useful in
PvE — **SLKR, GL Rey, Jabba, or JML** are the safest first-GL targets in 2026; **GL
Leia** a strong newer alternative. Avoid making **GL Ahsoka** or **SEE** a first GL
now.
