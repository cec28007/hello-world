# Decision Framework — "What should I do next?"

This file encodes how to answer prioritization questions for SWGOH so the advice
is consistent instead of re-derived each time. Pair it with the player profile
(`08-player-profile.md`) and the mechanics files.

## Mental model: one roster, many modes

GAC, Fleet Arena, Territory Wars, raids, and Era modes **all run on the same
roster**. So the question is never really "focus on GAC *or* fleet *or* Era" —
it's "which roster investments lift the most modes at once."

**GAC is the hub.** It has the best rewards (relic materials, Kyber, exclusive
shards via GAC/Aurodium currency), it rewards roster *depth*, and a GAC-strong
roster automatically carries fleet, TW, and raids. So:

- **GAC = the destination.** Optimize for it.
- **Fleet ranking = a feeder** worth one focused push (a strong fleet lifts both
  daily fleet-arena crystals *and* the GAC fleet zone).
- **Era ranking = a fuel depot, not a goal.** Its value is the Lightspeed Tokens
  you harvest and pour into your GAC teams. Don't chase the rank itself.
- **Daily squad + fleet arena climbs = the crystal engine** underneath
  everything. Hold the highest rank you comfortably can; crystals compound.

## The priority ladder (apply top-down)

1. **Finish Galactic Legends in progress.** A GL is the single biggest per-unit
   power lever (GAC offense + defense, TW, raids). Nearly always the top move.
2. **Finish anything ~90% done.** Sunk-cost-aware: a capital ship one ability
   from maxed, a GL a few tiers from unlock, a team missing one relic — closing
   these is cheap relative to the payoff.
3. **Fix the current pain point.** If the user keeps *losing GAC by a hair*, the
   fix is a defensive **wall that holds** (see below), not more offense.
4. **Build meta defensive walls** you nearly own (e.g., a faction where you have
   the whole team and just need gear/relics).
5. **Breadth / new factions from scratch** — lowest priority; only when it
   unlocks something specific and meta-relevant.

## The multi-mode test

Before committing resources to a unit/team, ask: **"Does this serve more than one
mode?"**

- Galactic Legends → GAC offense + GAC defense + TW + raids ✅✅✅
- A top capital ship (e.g., Profundity) → Fleet Arena + GAC fleet zone ✅✅
- A meta defensive faction (e.g., Great Mothers Nightsisters) → GAC defense + TW
  defense ✅✅
- A narrow, off-meta faction (e.g., Ewoks) → one niche use ❌ → back of the line

## GAC-specific: offense vs defense GL placement

Common newbie advice is "GLs on offense." That assumes your non-GL teams *can't*
clear the enemy's hardest squad. Check the actual situation:

- **If the player already full-clears opponents' boards** with non-GL teams,
  they don't need all GLs on offense. Their weakness is **holds**. → Put the best
  *defensive* GLs on defense (e.g., Rey, Leia), keep the best *offensive* GL
  (e.g., JML) for offense. One GL on offense usually cracks the enemy's single
  hardest team; deep relic squads clean up the rest.
- **A single successful defensive hold is worth more than winning the banner
  tiebreak.** That's why converting a loss-by-1 pattern means building holds.

See `01-gac.md` for why two full-clears can differ by one banner (efficiency:
surviving units + undersized bonuses), and `06-meta-teams.md` for wall choices.

## The energy-economy tradeoff (don't forget this)

Chasing a Galactic Legend means farming **Light or Dark side tickets**, which are
**energy-based drops**: you earn them by spending your *finite regular energy* on
Light/Dark battle nodes. Consequences (see `03-galactic-legends.md` for detail):

- **Same-side GLs compete** for the same ticket pool.
- **Opposite-side GLs don't** compete for tickets (separate Light vs Dark pools,
  144 cap each) — BUT both still pull from your **shared regular energy** and
  compete with **gear farming**.
- **Efficiency rule:** farm ticket nodes that *also* drop gear you need, and bank
  extra during **double-drop** events. Never spend energy purely on tickets when
  a dual-purpose node exists.

## How to answer a "what next" question (checklist)

1. Load/refresh the **player profile**. What GLs, fleets, and near-done projects
   exist? What's the stated pain point?
2. Apply the **priority ladder** and the **multi-mode test**.
3. Name the **single highest-ROI next move**, then the 2-3 that follow.
4. Call out any **resource tradeoff** (energy economy, currency, shard farms).
5. Flag **date-sensitive** meta claims and offer to verify.
6. Be decisive: give a recommendation and the reasoning, not an exhaustive
   survey.
